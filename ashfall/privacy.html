Ashfall Privacy Policy

Updated 27 October 2026

Data collection

Ashfall does not collect personal data, use advertising identifiers, or track you across apps or websites. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Saved game data

Game progress, scores, selected equipment, language, and control settings are stored locally on your device. Ashfall does not send this information to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include this information according to your Apple backup settings.

Permissions

The iPhone and iPad game uses touch and supported game controllers. The iOS app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store purchase records and diagnostics according to your account and device settings. These services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its local data in device Settings to remove the game’s saved progress and preferences. Backups are managed separately through Apple’s backup settings.

Contact and support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and message to respond to your request. Email contact@asapps.fr for support or privacy questions.

About this website

This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app. The support website is covered by the ASApps website privacy policy.
