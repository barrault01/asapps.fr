Is Tunzika free?

Yes. Live radio is free in your chosen country, with background playback and a sleep timer. The free tier includes 5 favorites and 3 song recognitions or album scans combined. Tunzika Pro lifts those limits and unlocks country changes and personalization.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

You don’t need Apple Music to listen to radio. Apple Music playback and the Blind Test quiz require authorized access and a compatible Apple Music subscription. Availability depends on your account and region.

Can I listen with my phone locked?

Yes. Radio keeps playing in the background, with controls on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. You can also use CarPlay, Siri shortcuts, and Apple Watch radio controls.

Can I listen offline?

Live radio needs an internet connection. Tunzika does not download radio streams or Apple Music tracks for offline listening.

Does my radio history sync across devices?

Your selected country, radio favorites, and radio history stay on your device. Tunzika doesn’t run an account or analytics backend. Optional recognition and music features contact Apple services, and stations receive standard network information when you connect.

Which languages does the app support?

Tunzika is available in English, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, and Italian on iPhone and iPad.
