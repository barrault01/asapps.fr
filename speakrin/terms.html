Your scripts and recordings

You keep your rights to your own content. Only import, record, and share material you are allowed to use, including other people’s dialogue and voices. Speakrin does not acquire ownership of your scripts or recordings.

Practice, review, and backups

Speech recognition and script-matching estimates can contain errors. Review your recordings and text before sharing. Keep copies of work you want to preserve; the app does not operate a recovery or cloud synchronization service. Available features depend on your device, system version, permissions, and installed language resources.
