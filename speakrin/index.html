Speakrin

A little text.
A lot of you.

For the speech you want to get right, the video you want to make, and the lines you want to remember.

01 / SPEAK

A script at your pace

Read aloud and let the text follow. Pause to breathe or pick up from a word with a tap. Use fixed-speed scrolling whenever you prefer.

  • Adjustable text & reading guide
  • Countdown & mirror mode
02 / RECORD

Make room for your message

Keep your script on screen while you record with the front or rear camera. Pause the text while the video keeps rolling.

  • Saved takes in the app
  • Watch & share when ready
03 / REHEARSE

Someone to run lines with

Choose your character. Speakrin speaks the other parts and waits for your reply. Hide your lines, reveal a hint, or use recorded dialogue.

  • Spoken scene partners
  • Your voice or a partner’s recording
Within speaking distance

Your scripts, close at hand.

Write a new script or import a TXT file, a PDF with selectable text, or a compatible EPUB. Your library and recordings stay on your device. Apple device backups may include app data, depending on your settings.
